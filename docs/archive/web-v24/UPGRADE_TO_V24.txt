Infinite Notes v24 upgrade

1. Export a .inotes backup.
2. Stop Infinite Notes with Ctrl+C.
3. Put infinite_notes_v24_patch.zip in the existing project folder.
4. Extract it:

   unzip -o infinite_notes_v24_patch.zip

5. Run:

   ./setup.sh --test
   ./run.sh

6. Fully close and reopen the iPad Safari tab or Home Screen app so it loads app.js?v=24.

v24 fixes
---------
- Rebuilt Pencil, finger, and selector routing at the pointer-event layer.
- Pen/highlighter input never hit-tests geometry unless the selector is active.
- One-finger geometry selection happens only after a stationary tap; finger motion pans instead.
- Tapping empty space after temporary selection restores the previous tool.
- Locking now applies to ink, text, and every geometry object.
- Locked items ignore direct taps and the eraser and remain lasso-recoverable.
- Tangent and normal snaps use a robust ellipse-boundary search.

The patch does not contain data/ and does not overwrite the active PDF or notes.
