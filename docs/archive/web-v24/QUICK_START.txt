INFINITE NOTES V24 — QUICK START

ONE-TIME SETUP

  chmod +x run.sh setup.sh diagnose.sh install-desktop-shortcut.sh
  ./setup.sh --test

NORMAL START

  ./run.sh

This starts NotesHotspot, starts Infinite Notes, opens the desktop interface,
prints the iPad address, and shuts down both when you press Ctrl+C.

GEOMETRY

  1. Select the Shapes icon.
  2. Pick a shape and drag to create it.
  3. Tap and release unlocked geometry with one finger to select it temporarily.
  4. Use Apple Pencil in the selector to tap/drag geometry directly.
  5. Tap empty space after a finger selection to return to the previous tool.
  6. Use selector handles to scale/rotate; line and curve points have handles.
  7. Lasso-select any ink, text, or geometry and press lock to protect it.
  8. Locked x-y planes remain directly above the PDF background.

HOLD RECOGNITION

  Draw with the pen, keep the Pencil down, and pause. A sufficiently straight
  stroke becomes a line; a sufficiently smooth bend becomes a quadratic curve.
  Thresholds and snapping controls are in Settings.

SERVER WITHOUT THE HOTSPOT

  ./run.sh --no-hotspot

DIAGNOSTICS

  ./diagnose.sh

Your current PDF, pages, ink, text, and geometry are stored under data/.
